FIELD NOTES / WHAT MOVES THE SCORE

  • Plain bullets beat numbered lists.
  • Short headers win. Drop the (18 ideas) tail.
  • = separators help. Literal *** hurts.
  • Scores wobble. Check five times, keep the lowest.
  • Whole-document score is not the mean of sections.
